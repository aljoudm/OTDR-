# Captions for the background figures (research paper)

Each entry: image file, then the caption.

### `figures/das_timeline.pdf` / `figures/das_timeline.png`
**Development of distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) as three steps.** Adapted from Shang et al., Sensors 2022, Fig. 1. The double line climbs through the qualitative stage (grey: 1976 OTDR, 1982 COTDR, 1993 Φ-OTDR, 2005 first intrusion-detection use), the quantitative stage (blue: 2013 3×3, 2015 PGC and 2016 IQ demodulation, which recover the vibration waveform) and the performance-enhancement stage (coral: 2017 ultrafast laser for sensitivity, 2018 UWFBG for frequency response, 2021 FDM against coherent fading). Each circle marks one milestone and its label sits directly above its own stem; the stage colours are the same as in the system diagram. Script: `scripts/fig_das_timeline.m`.

### `figures/das_evolution.pdf` / `figures/das_evolution.png`
**Evolution OTDR → Φ-OTDR → COTDR, built in Simulink** (adapted from Shang et al., Sensors 2022, Figs. 2-4). The three panels share one column grid, so the same component sits in the same column in every panel. Light blue: inherited from the previous panel; coral with a thick border: new in this panel. (a) OTDR: a pulsed laser, EDFA (+10 dB), circulator (0.8 dB), the fibre under test (2 km SMF, blue) and a power photodetector placed below the circulator; the backscatter returns from the circulator to the detector. (b) Φ-OTDR: a narrow-linewidth laser (NLL, 5 kHz) and an acousto-optic modulator (AOM, 100 ns) replace the pulsed laser, so the probe pulses are coherent and the detected intensity depends on the fibre phase. (c) COTDR: a 99:1 coupler taps 1 % of the laser as local oscillator (LO), which travels along the bottom to a second coupler where it beats with the backscatter; the I/Q photodetector then delivers the complex signal from which the vibration phase is recovered. Each component is a masked Subsystem containing the corresponding `das_model` blocks (gains g_edfa, g_cir, sqrt(k_sig), sqrt(k_lo); pulse frame p_frame; the NLL random-walk phase; the MATLAB Function fibre models). Presentation simplifications: the circulator is drawn forward-only with its return pass shown as the line leaving its bottom, and the coupler-2 beat product E_R·conj(LO) stands in for the optical mixing of the physical receiver. Model: `model/das_evolution.slx`; script: `scripts/build_das_evolution.m`.
